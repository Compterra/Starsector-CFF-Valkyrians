Location: ES-19J
Date: 208.07.22

After the alien mothership was destroyed, the {Valkyrians} went quiet. Their shipyard assembly lines stopped. Workers and equipment were redirected into sealed logistics channels, though the destination of the shipments remained unclear.

The Hegemony space marshal responsible for the region studied the reports and suspected there was more behind the silence.

He was right.

The marshal increased reconnaissance. His forces had been reduced several times and were formally on the defensive, but scouting and probing continued at wartime tempo.

Months later, a small Hegemony strike force detected an enormous super-battleship in the debris field. All available readings suggested construction was nearly complete.

The Hegemony immediately mobilized the major combat ships still assigned to the Valkyrian front for a destruction mission. When they arrived, the giant and its support fleet were already waiting.

The first salvo turned the debris field white.
