Location: Unknown nebula
Date: 174.03.26

Kanako was a strikingly capable captain whose service ended when she began to reject the brutality of the {Valkyrians}. She was stripped of command and sentenced to permanent confinement.

A former marine-lancer does not stay in prison easily. Kanako escaped, reached a shuttle port, and hid inside a cargo container bound for a naval assembly plant.

Fortune arrived exactly when she needed it. She seized a completed Nirvana Mk.II from the line and fled {Valkyria} Station.

The escape did not clear the patrol net. Several nearby patrol ships were ordered to pursue. Kanako powered her flagship down, slipped into a nebula, and tried to bury her radar return and engine wake.

Now a Jenova frigate equipped with advanced sensors has locked onto an anomalous emission source.

Can she escape?
