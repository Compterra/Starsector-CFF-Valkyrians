Location: Unknown nebula
Date: 174.03.26

Kanako was a strikingly capable captain whose service ended when she began to reject the brutality of the {Valkyrians}. She was stripped of command and sentenced to permanent confinement.

A former foot-lancer does not stay in prison easily. Kanako escaped, reached a shuttle port, and hid inside a cargo container loaded onto a tractor. The container carried her into a naval assembly plant.

Fortune arrived exactly when she needed it. She seized a completed Nirvana Mk.II from the line and fled {Valkyria} Station.

The escape did not avoid the patrol net. Several nearby patrol ships were ordered to follow. Kanako powered her flagship down, slipped into a nebula, and tried to bury her radar return and engine wake.

Unfortunately, a Jenova frigate equipped with advanced sensors has locked onto an anomalous emission source...

Can she escape?
