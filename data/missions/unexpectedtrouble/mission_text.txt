Location: NW-3B
Date: 201.02.07

The newly developed {VKS Valkyria}, a {Yoshura} Mk.II-class ship, recently launched on her maiden voyage to the front in the Ophiuchus system. After encountering what appeared to be a jump interdiction field, Valkyria and her escort formation were forced out of hyperspace.

Unfortunately, they emerged in the middle of a massive pirate fleet group. Hundreds of ships flying red skull banners circled a pirate base.

The escort commander issued clear orders: half the warships would form a ring line to drive off the pirates while the rest withdrew with the escorted capital. But the captain of {VKS Valkyria}, seized by heroic impulse, ordered the flagship to make a tactical burn at maximum thrust instead of shifting into travel configuration.

{VKS Valkyria} surged ahead and left the withdrawal column. The commander had no choice but to cancel acceleration and order all ships to spread out and join the battle.

The entire retreating formation turned toward the pirate fleet's left flank.

They would have to defend everything they had.
