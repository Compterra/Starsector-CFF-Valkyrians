Location: NW-3B
Date: 201.02.07

The newly developed {VKS Valkyria}, a {Yoshura} Mk.II-class ship, has launched on her maiden voyage to the front in the Ophiuchus system. After encountering what appears to be a jump interdiction field, Valkyria and her escort formation are forced out of hyperspace.

They emerge in the middle of a massive pirate fleet. Hundreds of ships flying red skull banners circle a pirate base.

The escort commander issues clear orders: half the warships will form a ring line to drive off the pirates while the rest withdraw with the escorted capital. But the captain of {VKS Valkyria}, seized by a heroic impulse, orders the flagship into a maximum-thrust tactical burn instead of shifting to travel configuration.

{VKS Valkyria} surges ahead and leaves the withdrawal column. The commander has no choice but to cancel acceleration and order all ships to spread out and join the battle.

The entire retreating formation turns toward the pirate fleet's left flank.

They will have to hold with everything they have.
