Location: Planetary orbit near Heaven's Gate
Date: 213.04.02

{Heaven's Gate} is a metastable natural wormhole on the outer edge of {Valkyrian} space.

During the early development of the region, this short-range passage served as the economic engine behind the expansion of a mining empire. The Collapse changed that. Famine, endless deflation, and corporate overreach shattered the giants that had once ruled the area. Not long afterward, the wandering {Valkyrians} intervened and took control.

Several military operations connected to the {Atlantis Nebula} later proved the strategic value of {Heaven's Gate}. Rivalry with greater powers forced the {Valkyrians} to maintain a large fleet, allowing them to hold the wormhole while remaining competitive in external wars. The orbital base fleet under the Governor of Heaven's Gate and the royal fleet guarding Enigma together consumed nearly sixty percent of military expenditures.

...

Time passed. Modernizing an old military state strained the national reserves to the breaking point. A rebellion triggered a fiscal crisis, sharpened tensions among army, government, and civilians, and finally erupted into civil war.

Garrisons across Valkyrian space were recalled. A Hegemony space marshal saw the weakened frontier and committed regular fleets and mercenaries to strike {Valkyria} on several fronts. Most attacks were repelled within three days, but a Hegemony war fleet hid inside the storm of assaults and drove straight for {Heaven's Gate}.

The new queen's flagship was still in hyperspace when she ordered the Governor of Heaven's Gate to abandon the stronghold and join the royal battle plan.

The governor refused. Her stand defended both the honor of {Valkyria} and the ancient privileges that kept the Heaven's Gate region independent of direct royal authority. The Hegemony's ambition was stopped, but the governor died in battle. With its military power nearly spent, Heaven's Gate was swept into the final phase of modernization. Royal Ordinance Twelve abolished the Governorate of Heaven's Gate, revoked its tax privileges, economic allocations, and autonomous statutes, and placed the region under joint protection of the Second Royal Guard and the planetary defense fleet.
