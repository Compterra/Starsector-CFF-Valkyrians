Location: Planetary orbit near Heaven's Gate
Date: 213.04.02

{Heaven's Gate} is a metastable natural wormhole on the outer edge of {Valkyrian} space. Short-ranged and difficult to navigate, it is still valuable enough to build a state around.

Before the Collapse, the passage fed a mining empire. Afterward, famine, deflation, and corporate predation broke that empire apart. The wandering {Valkyrians} arrived in the ruins and made the gate their frontier shield.

Wars around the {Atlantis Nebula} later proved its strategic worth. Holding the wormhole required a permanent battlefleet, and the old Governorate of Heaven's Gate became almost a state within the state: loyal to the crown in war, jealous of its ancient rights in peace.

For decades, that arrangement held.

Then modernization began. Refitting an aging military state drained the treasury, strained the army, and turned civilian resentment into revolt. A rebellion became a fiscal crisis; the crisis became a civil war.

Garrisons were recalled from across Valkyrian space. A Hegemony space marshal saw the weakened frontier and committed regular fleets and mercenaries to strike {Valkyria} on several fronts. Most attacks were repelled within three days, but one Hegemony war fleet used the chaos as cover and drove straight for {Heaven's Gate}.

The new queen's flagship was still in hyperspace when she ordered the Governor of Heaven's Gate to abandon the stronghold and join the royal battle plan.

The governor refused.

Her fleet held the line, defending both {Valkyria}'s honor and the ancient privileges that kept Heaven's Gate beyond direct royal authority. The Hegemony advance was stopped, but the governor died with her command. When the battle ended, the Governorate's military power was spent. Royal Ordinance Twelve soon abolished its special rights and placed the region under joint protection of the Second Royal Guard and the planetary defense fleet.

This is the battle that ended the old frontier.
