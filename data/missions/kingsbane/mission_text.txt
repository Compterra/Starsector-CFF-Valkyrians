Location: Deep space
Date: 207.05.07

A battle has broken out between the {Valkyrians} and {Blackrock Drive Yards}. Valkyrian command will not tolerate a rival power they consider an imitator operating near one of their high-tech mining fleets.

VKS {Exile}, a {Valkyrian} super-battleship, holds the rear of the line, protecting the fleet and anchoring morale.

What Valkyrian command does not know is that their opponent has hired mercenaries specifically to kill the giant. If {VKS Exile} becomes separated from the main body, the strike fleet will move in.

{VKS Exile} is battle-ready and supported by an understrength but heavily armed escort group. Both sides enter the battle convinced they have already found the winning move.
