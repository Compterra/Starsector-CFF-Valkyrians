Location: Deep space
Date: 207.05.07

A battle has broken out between the {Valkyrians} and {Blackrock Drive Yards}. The Valkyrians will not tolerate a rival power they consider a mere imitator skulking around their high-tech mining fleet.

VKS {Exile}, a {Valkyrian} super-battleship, holds the rear of the line, protecting the fleet and anchoring morale.

What Valkyrian command does not know is that their opponent has hired mercenaries specifically to attack the giant. If {VKS Exile} becomes separated from the main body, the mercenary fleet will strike and clip {Valkyria}'s wings.

{VKS Exile} is in good condition and supported by an incomplete but heavily armed escort group. Both sides enter the battle with full confidence.
